Clarification of Title 30-A Chapter  223 Section 5823

The intent of Title 30-A Chapter 223 Section 5823 is to document in Maine State law, that government auditing standards promulgated by the U.S. Government Accountability Office must be used when required by the U.S. government, or when required by an authority other than Maine’s Office of the State Auditor.
